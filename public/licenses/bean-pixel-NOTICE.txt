Bean Pixel Regular

An English/Latin subset of Fusion Pixel 12px Monospaced, release 2026.09.01.
Subsetting and font naming changes by BEAN LABS, 2026-09-24.
Original glyph shapes are unmodified. Internal family and PostScript names
are changed to Bean Pixel / BeanPixel-Regular to respect Reserved Font Names.
